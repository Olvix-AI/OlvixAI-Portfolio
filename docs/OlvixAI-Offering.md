# OlvixAI — What We Offer

## In one line
We build AI-powered products from scratch, and we add AI features into software that already exists — including everything needed to actually run it in production.

## What we actually do (two things)

### 1. New Builds
A founder or company has an idea for an AI-powered product (an app, a tool, a platform).
We take it from idea → working product → live, production-ready launch.

This includes:
- Product design (UI/UX)
- Frontend (web + mobile)
- Backend & the AI/agent logic itself
- Production setup (hosting, infrastructure, monitoring, security)

### 2. AI Integration
A company already has a working website, app, or SaaS product — but no AI features (or weak ones).
We add AI capabilities into what they already have: chatbots, automation, AI agents, smart features — without rebuilding their whole product.

## What makes us different
Most small AI teams can build an impressive demo. Very few can also make it reliable, secure, and stable once real users show up.
We do both — the AI/product work **and** the DevOps/infrastructure work that makes it actually hold up in production, under one team.

## Who we work with
- Funded startups building an AI-powered product
- Existing SaaS/software companies who want to add AI features
- Open to local and international clients (leaning toward US/EU)

## Team
Four engineers covering AI, web development, mobile development, and DevOps/cloud — one team, full-stack, no need to hire separately for each layer.
